# Project Browser 0.8.1

Motion polish for the tab strip, and the parts of four features that were still missing.

## Features completed

- **Dark pages: Off / Auto / Force** (Settings > Appearance). Auto asks websites for their own dark theme
  (`prefers-color-scheme: dark`). Force also darkens pages that are still bright; pictures and video keep their colours,
  and pages that are already dark are left alone. A **moon button** for the toolbar (add it with Customize) switches the
  current site on or off; a second click restores the original look. Per-site choices are remembered and are in backups.
- **Your own address-bar shortcuts** (Settings > General > Search): `!key words` searches any site you add, before the
  built-in ones. `!wiki` joins the built-ins (`!yt`, `!maps`, `!gh`, `!r`, `!a` and more).
- **Notes:** an **All notes** view in the Notes panel with search, delete and **Export** (a text file you save where you
  like). Notes are now part of Backup and restore.
- **Link Cleaner:** each cleaned address shows **Undo** (load the original address once) and **Never on this site**;
  a list of sites to leave alone is in Settings > Privacy.

## Motion

- **Closing a tab** no longer makes the strip jump: the tab collapses where it was and its neighbours slide into the gap.
- **Switching tabs:** the active tab's background and text settle in smoothly instead of snapping.
- **Loading:** when a page finishes loading, its site icon fades in where the spinner was.
- **Pressing** a tab gives a slight press-in; the new-tab button turns as you press it.
- **Right-click menu:** its content fades in as the menu window appears.

All of it uses short transform and opacity animations (the close collapse also animates the tab's width for 0.17 s),
follows Settings > Appearance > Motion, and is off when motion is off or Windows asks for reduced motion.
