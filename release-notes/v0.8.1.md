# Project Browser 0.8.1

Motion polish for the tab strip (the part of 0.8.0's design work that did not make it into that release).

- **Closing a tab** no longer makes the strip jump: the tab collapses where it was and its neighbours slide into the gap.
- **Switching tabs:** the active tab's background and text settle in smoothly instead of snapping.
- **Loading:** when a page finishes loading, its site icon fades in where the spinner was.
- **Pressing** a tab gives a slight press-in; the new-tab button turns as you press it.
- **Right-click menu:** its content fades in as the menu window appears.

All of it uses short transform and opacity animations (the close collapse also animates the tab's width for 0.17 s),
follows Settings > Appearance > Motion, and is off when motion is off or Windows asks for reduced motion.
