# Project Browser 0.8.2

A stabilization release: the right-click menu works every time, and the interface animations are actually there.

## Fixed

- **Right-click worked once, then stopped.** Your log showed why: after the first menu, WebView2 moved keyboard focus
  away from the menu within a few milliseconds; 0.8.1 took focus back, which moved it again, three times, and then the
  menu gave up and closed (about 60 ms after opening). The menu window no longer takes keyboard focus at all, like a
  system menu: the page keeps focus, and the menu closes when you click outside it, pick an entry, press Escape, scroll,
  or switch to another program. Escape and the arrow keys (with Enter) work while the page has focus.
- **Animations seemed to be missing.** With Gaming Mode on and its "Reduce animations" option (both on by default in
  many profiles), every interface animation was switched off completely, even with Motion set to Expressive. Gaming
  Mode now makes motion *subtle* (shorter, smaller) instead; Settings > Appearance says when it does. Motion "Off", the
  Performance theme and Windows' "Animation effects" setting still turn it off.

## Motion

- New Tab tiles, settings sections, panel content and download rows rise in one after another; tiles lift on hover.
- Buttons, sidebar entries and panel rows press in; toggles slide; the address bar's focus ring eases in; settings
  sections slide in when you switch; side panels open from the edge; toolbar badges pop when they change.
- All of it animates only transform, opacity and colours, follows Settings > Appearance > Motion
  (Subtle / Standard / Expressive / Off) and Windows' reduced-motion setting.

## Tested

- 30 consecutive right-click cycles in the real app on a Windows machine (page, link, selected text, text field;
  closed by Escape, a click outside and the keyboard; with a toolbar popup, a second tab and a window resize in between).
- Tab dragging, downloads (all fixtures, three configurations) and startup checks as in 0.8.0.
